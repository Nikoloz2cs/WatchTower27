# WatchTower27 Support

Welcome to the support page for **WatchTower27**! Below you'll find helpful information, frequently asked questions, and ways to contact us for further assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I report a parking lot sighting?
- Open the app and ensure location services are enabled.
- Tap on the map pin of a parking lot and submit your sighting.

### 2. Why can't I submit a report?
- Ensure that you are within the bounds of a valid location for reporting, or have selected the option "report for a friend".
- Check that your location services are enabled for WatchTower27 in your device settings.

### 3. How do I verify my email?
- After signing up, a verification email will be sent to your inbox. Click on the link in the email to verify your account.
